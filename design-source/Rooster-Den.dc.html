The Rooster Den Co

A cowgirl-chic Shopify redesign for a woman-owned western boutique in White Rock, with clearer shopping journeys, page-by-page SEO and a Google Business Profile.

The Story

It started with SEO. It became a whole new store.

The owner first came to me for SEO, because her Shopify admin was showing that it hadn’t been set up.

After we talked and she saw my work, she asked me to redesign the whole store as well. I took on the redesign and the SEO, and set up her Google Business Profile too.

SEO and Google Business Profile

Easier to find, page by page

Every page has its own search-friendly title and description, written around what shoppers look for, from western handbags to statement jewelry.

I also set up her Google Business Profile, so the boutique can be found on Google Search and Maps.
