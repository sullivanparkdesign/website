SEO and GEO

The same foundation, two kinds of search

Traditional SEO helps your pages appear in search results. GEO, or generative engine optimization, helps AI tools understand your business. Both rely on clear, well-organized and genuinely useful content.

Traditional SEO
FocusKeywords, page structure, content and site health
Where it appliesSearch results and map listings
Generative engine optimization
FocusClear answers, structured information and well-organized content
Where it appliesAI-generated answers, such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI results
