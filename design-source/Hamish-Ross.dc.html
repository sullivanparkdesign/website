Portfolio · Real Estate

Hamish Ross Real Estate

A custom-coded website for a White Rock and South Surrey realtor, designed to feel as considered as the homes he represents.

Visit Website
Client
Hamish Ross, Macdonald Realty
Location
White Rock & South Surrey, BC
What I Did
Website design and build · Custom IDX search · SEO · Ongoing marketing
Platform
Custom-coded website

The Brief

Big, luxurious photography. Smarter ways to search.

Hamish came to me wanting a website that felt slick and modern, with big, luxurious photography and a better way for his clients to search than a standard real estate site allows.

He specializes in family, luxury and ocean view homes, so every page needed to feel as considered as the properties he represents, while still making it easy for buyers and sellers to find what they came for.

  1. 01Visual design and a custom-coded build
  2. 02Custom IDX search collections
  3. 03Property and market resources
  4. 04SEO, blogs, social media and newsletters

The Homepage

Start to finish, like a good showing

The homepage unfolds in the order a buyer or seller needs it, from a striking first impression to every way to search.

Custom IDX Search

Search the way buyers actually think

Hamish wanted his website to highlight the features his clients ask about most, like pools and ocean views, in a way standard templates couldn’t.

I customized the IDX integration with the MLS® system so homes like these have collections of their own, updated automatically and easy to browse.

Collections include

  • Ocean View Homes
  • Homes with Pools
  • Estates and Acreage
  • Penthouses

On Every Screen

Just as considered on a phone

Many buyers browse homes on their phones, so I designed every page to feel just as calm and easy to use on a small screen.

Beyond the Launch

Marketing that keeps going

The website was only the beginning. I continue to look after the marketing for Hamish’s listings, so each home is presented with the same care online, in inboxes and on social media.

  • Newsletters, social media and ads for each of Hamish’s listings
  • Marketing updates for sellers on the 1st and 15th of every month, showing the work done
  • SEO and blog content that keeps the website useful and easy to find
