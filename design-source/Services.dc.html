How It Works

From first conversation to finished work

We’ll establish what you need, agree on the scope and work through the details together. The approach is shaped around your project, whether that’s a website, marketing materials or ongoing support.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    We discuss your business, your goals and where you need help. You don’t need a finished brief or technical knowledge to get started.

  2. 02

    A clear plan

    I outline the proposed work, pricing and next steps, so we have a shared understanding of the scope before work begins.

  3. 03

    Creation and refinement

    I develop the agreed design, content or SEO work, with opportunities for your feedback at the relevant stages.

  4. 04

    Delivery and ongoing support

    Depending on the project, we launch your website, deliver your materials or implement the agreed updates. If you need continued support, we can agree on a separate ongoing scope.
