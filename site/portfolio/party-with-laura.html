The Story

Four ways to create. One place to start.

Laura Webb is a certified Bob Ross landscape and floral instructor with more than 15 years of teaching experience. From her home studio in Cloverdale, she runs weekly classes, paint parties, workshops and summer camps, and brings the studio to homes, offices and community events across the Fraser Valley.

Her business was already well established. What she needed was a website that felt as inspiring as her classes, explained each program clearly, made booking simple and helped local families find her.

  1. 01Website design, built from scratch
  2. 02Copywriting for every page
  3. 03SEO and local search
  4. 04Booking, enquiries and gift certificates

The Homepage

Bright, welcoming and easy to follow

The homepage introduces Laura, shows every program at a glance and always makes the next step clear.

  2. IIWelcome. Discover your inner artist
  3. IIICreative art experiences for all ages

  5. VReady for your next creative adventure?

Four Programs

Four programs, four clear paths

Weekly classes, paint parties, summer camps and workshops each serve a different crowd, from young artists to bachelorette parties. Each one has its own card, its own page and its own next step, so every visitor can find the right fit.

  • Weekly Classes Ages 6 to 18Small groups of 2 to 5September to June
  • Paint Parties Birthdays, bachelorettes and team eventsGuided, no experience neededAt her studio or yours
  • Summer Camps Ages 7 and upSmall groups of 3 to 6Half-day or full-day
  • Workshops Themed weekend projectsBob Ross oil paintingAcrylics and mixed media

SEO and Local Search

Found close to home

Every page has its own search-friendly title and description, written around what local families look for, with Cloverdale, Surrey, Langley and the Fraser Valley woven naturally into the copy.

I also tidied up the page addresses and moved the site onto her own domain for launch.

A few of the page titles and descriptions

  • partywithlaura.com › weekly-classes Weekly Kids Art Classes | Small Group Lessons in Cloverdale Personalized art instruction for kids and teens (ages 6–18). Small class sizes (2–5 students) ensure individual guidance in painting, sketching, and mixed media.
  • partywithlaura.com › paint-parties Private Paint Parties & Team Building | Mobile Art Studio BC Host an unforgettable paint party! Perfect for birthdays, bachelorettes, and corporate events. We bring supplies and guided instruction to your location.
  • partywithlaura.com › summer-camps Kids Creative Art Summer Camps 2026 | Cloverdale & Surrey Register for boutique summer art camps for ages 7+. Small groups (3–6 students) mean personalized fun in painting, clay, and crafts. Registration opens in May!
