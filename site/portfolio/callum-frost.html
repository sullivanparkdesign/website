Callum Frost

A classic black and white website for a Vancouver actor and voice-over artist, rebuilt from Squarespace in custom code, with film-inspired details that let his headshots take the lead.

The Story

Classy, simple and all about him

When Callum was starting out as an actor, his family asked me to build him a website. At the time, the goal was to give agents a polished, professional place to see his work.

We talked it through together. Callum wanted something classy and simple, so we chose black and white and let his headshots do the talking. He now has an agent.

His first site was built on Squarespace. As his career grew, I rebuilt it in custom code, keeping the look he loves and adding more polish to every page.

  1. 01Website design, now custom-coded
  2. 02Headshot direction
  3. 03His acting resume and all the copy
  4. 04SEO, domain and ongoing updates

Version 2.0

Everything he loved, with more polish

Callum loved his original site, so the new version keeps the same pages, colours, photos and style. Rebuilding it in custom code let me add film-inspired details and a few useful new features.

  1. f.01 Film-inspired details A running timecode on the homepage, film-strip frames around his photos and a soft film reel shadow.
  2. f.02 A photo viewer Every photo opens larger, with arrows, thumbnails and keyboard controls for browsing.
  3. f.03 A gallery for each show His theatre productions each have their own gallery, alongside his headshots and film and TV photos.
  4. f.04 A resume to share Visitors can download or print his resume right from the page.

The Homepage

One look, then straight to the work

The homepage opens on a full-width black and white headshot with a running timecode, then leads visitors to everything an agent or casting director needs.

The Details

Black and white, nothing to distract

A quiet palette of charcoal, grey and silver keeps every page calm and classic, so the focus stays on Callum, with a small red “rec” light on the homepage. Lowercase headings in Roboto feel modern and understated, and Roboto Mono adds the film details.

Behind the Scenes

More than a website

An actor’s website is only as good as the photos and resume behind it, so I helped with those too, and I still keep everything up to date.

  1. 01 Headshot direction Before his headshot session, I gave advice on the kinds of photos I wanted for the site, so the shoot and the design worked together.
  2. 02 Resume and copy I built his acting resume and wrote all the copy on the site.
  3. 03 Ongoing updates I look after the site for him. When he has new photos or resume changes, I add them.
  4. 04 SEO and domain I set up the SEO, helped him buy his domain and was there with advice along the way.
