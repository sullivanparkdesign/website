SMASH Performing Arts Studio

A custom-coded website for a musical theatre studio in South Surrey and White Rock, with an AI assistant for families and a members’ portal for everything between classes.

Visit Website
Client
SMASH Performing Arts Studio
Location
South Surrey & White Rock, BC
What I Did
Website design and build · AI assistant · Members’ portal · SEO · Newsletters
Platform
Custom-coded website

The Brief

A lot to share. Easy to find.

SMASH runs full-year programs, 10-week sessions, camps, dance classes and four musicals a season, so families have a lot to keep track of.

I designed and built a new custom-coded website that makes each program easy to understand and gives families clear answers, from their very first visit to show week.

  1. 01Visual design and a custom-coded build
  2. 02Ask SMASH, an AI assistant for families
  3. 03A password-protected members’ portal
  4. 04SEO, blogs, social media and newsletters

The Homepage

A season, in six acts

The homepage unfolds like a show program, from the opening scene to the moment a family decides to join.

  2. Act IIThe 2026/27 season
  3. Act IIIPrograms and classes

The Details

Midnight navy, gold and spotlight blue

Deep navy sets the stage, gold adds a touch of theatre and spotlight blue lights up the key words. Cormorant Garamond brings the drama to headings, and Jost keeps everything else clear and friendly.

Ask SMASH

Answers for families, any time of day

Families have a lot of questions, from trial periods to show dates. Ask SMASH is an AI assistant built into the website to help them find answers about the studio and its programs, reducing the need for repetitive email enquiries.

Each answer points families to the right page or contact, so they always know where to go next.

Members’ Portal

Everything families need, behind one password

A password-protected portal gives families show details, vocal tracks, important dates, studio policies and newsletters in one place, organized by their performer’s level.

  • Show details
  • Vocal tracks
  • Dates to remember
  • Payment schedule
  • Studio policies
  • Newsletters

Monthly Newsletters

News that feels like part of the show

I design SMASH’s monthly newsletters to match the new website, so every issue feels like part of the same story, with the dates, shows and studio news families need.

Blog Templates

A template for every kind of post

I designed four blog templates for SMASH, each shaped around a different kind of post, so a show announcement, a class guide and studio news each feel distinct while still clearly belonging to SMASH.

A Sample of the Pages

Every program, its own spotlight

Each program, show and schedule has a page of its own, so families can quickly find what fits their performer.

On Every Screen

Just as considered on a phone

Families often check schedules and show details on the go, so I designed every page to feel just as clear on a small screen.
