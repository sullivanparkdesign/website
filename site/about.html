About Sullivan Park Design

An eye for design. A practical understanding of your business.

I’m Natasha, the founder and designer behind Sullivan Park Design. I create websites, develop a consistent visual presence and help businesses improve how they’re found in search.

My background in the creative arts shapes how I see composition, colour and detail. My work in marketing and SEO shapes the questions I ask: Is the message clear? Can visitors find what they need? Does the design help them take the next step?

I bring that creative and practical perspective to Squarespace, Shopify and custom-coded websites, branding and ongoing marketing.

Seeing how the pieces fit together

Your website doesn’t exist in isolation. The way your business looks, the words you use and the materials you share all contribute to how people understand and remember you.

I work across those connections, from website layouts and service descriptions to search content, social graphics and brochures. That broader view helps keep your message and visual identity consistent wherever customers encounter your business.

My projects span real estate, the performing arts, learning and wellness. Each starts with understanding the business and the people it needs to reach.

A clear, collaborative way of working

You know your business. My role is to ask useful questions, offer clear recommendations and help translate your ideas into design and content that serve it well.

You’ll work directly with me, with someone who understands the details and how they connect to the wider project. You don’t need to arrive with a finished brief or know the technical terminology. We can work through the choices together, starting with what you need most.
