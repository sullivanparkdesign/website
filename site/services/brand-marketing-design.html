What It Covers

From direction to finished materials

The work can start with a new direction or build on the look you already have. We’ll agree on the specific pieces as part of the project scope.

  • Visual brand direction

    What it is

    A clear direction for how your business looks and feels, based on how you want it to be seen.

    Where it shows up

    Your website, social content and marketing materials.

  • Colour and typography

    What it is

    A palette and type pairing chosen to work together.

    Where it shows up

    On screen and in print.

  • Social graphics and ads

    What it is

    Designs for social content and ads that clearly belong to the same business.

    Where it shows up

    Social media and online ads.

  • Newsletter and print design

    What it is

    Newsletter layouts and printed materials that carry the same visual direction.

    Where it shows up

    Email newsletters and printed materials.

This service covers visual direction and marketing design. It doesn’t include logo creation.

Where You’re Starting

Two ways to begin

Finding a clear direction

If your materials feel inconsistent, or you’re not sure how your business should look, we start by defining your colours, typography and imagery.

Carrying an established look forward

If you already have a logo and a look you like, I can carry it into everyday marketing, from social posts and ads to newsletters and print.

Social content and marketing design can also be part of ongoing support.
