Platforms

Choosing the right platform

The right choice depends on what your business needs, how you’ll manage the site and the features involved. We’ll talk through the options before deciding on a direction.

  • Squarespace

    See Squarespace work

    Best for

    Service businesses that want a polished site they can update themselves.

    Good to know

    You can handle everyday changes yourself, such as changing text, swapping photos or adding blog posts.

  • Shopify

    See Shopify work

    Best for

    Online stores that need products, payments and shipping managed in one place.

    Good to know

    Built for selling online, so your store and its day-to-day running live in one system.

  • Custom-coded

    See Custom-coded work

    Best for

    Projects that need full design freedom or features beyond a template.

    Good to know

    Editing options depend on how the site is built, so we’ll plan that together before starting.
