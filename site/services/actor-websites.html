How It Works

From headshots to live site

  1. 01Choose your lookPick Luminous, Editorial or Headliner, and the colourway you like best.
  2. 02Send me your materialsYour headshots, resume, reel links and a short bio. I’ll help you organize them.
  3. 03I build your siteI set up your pages, connect your domain and make sure everything is ready for search.
  4. 04Keep it current yourselfUpdate your headshots, credits and bio whenever you book something new.

Building for a young actor?

I keep personal phone numbers, emails and home addresses off the site and use your agent’s details instead. If you’d like a contact form, I set it up in a parent’s name, so messages go to you.
