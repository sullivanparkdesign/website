About these terms

These terms are the agreement between you and me, Natasha Young, operating as Sullivan Park Design. Your order confirmation and these terms together make up our agreement. If they ever differ, your order confirmation applies.

“You” means the customer named on your order confirmation. “I” and “me” mean Natasha Young.

Who the customer is

  • If the actor is 19 or older, the actor is the customer, even if a family member pays.
  • If the actor is under 19 (a minor in BC), a parent or legal guardian must be the customer. The parent or guardian reviews and accepts these terms, pays, and approves what goes on the website.

What’s included

Your website is built for you, and you can update its everyday content yourself. Additional help and design changes are quoted separately, subject to availability. Hosting is currently included under the hosting terms.

Website setup: $650 CAD plus GST. It includes:

  • One of my three actor website designs (Luminous, Editorial or Headliner), in one of its three colourways.
  • The pages that come with your chosen design, set up with the materials you supply.
  • Your Resume page, filled in from the details or resume PDF you supply. I leave off private details such as personal phone numbers and home addresses.
  • Your bio: a short bio I write from your notes, or your finished bio added as you supply it.
  • One consolidated revision round before launch (see Preview, revisions and launch).
  • Connecting your domain, which you buy and own in your name.
  • If you’d like a contact form, connecting it to a Formspree account in your name.
  • Your own edit screen, for updating your everyday content within your chosen design: headshots and production stills, reels, credits, training, skills, bio, resume PDF, name and personal details, agent and contact details, social links and the short wording on your pages.

Optional: custom colours and fonts, an additional $75 CAD plus GST. I set up your design in one coordinated colour palette and one font pairing, instead of one of the standard colourways.

Not included unless I quote it separately:

  • A custom resume document, or copywriting beyond your bio
  • Photo retouching or editing
  • Help, design changes or other work after launch, including extra pages, new features or a different design
  • Changes beyond the one revision round
  • Your domain’s registration and renewal fees
  • Paid plans with outside services such as Formspree, YouTube or Vimeo

I always confirm the scope and price of extra work before I start. Extra work is subject to my availability.

Price and payment

Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus GST. Your order confirmation shows the itemized price, the GST and the total.

  • Initial payment: half of your full total, including any extras and GST. It reserves my agreed project time for you and is applied towards your website price.
  • Balance: due before launch. The other half is due once you’ve approved your website and before it goes live at your domain.
  • How to pay: by Interac e-Transfer, using the details on your invoice. I send an invoice for each payment.

I won’t add a charge that isn’t on your order confirmation unless you’ve agreed to it in writing, such as extra work I’ve quoted.

Timeline and late materials

Your dates. Before you pay, we agree a start date. Your order confirmation records it, along with the date your website will be ready for your review and your expected launch date. Allow two weeks from the start date, once I’ve received your initial payment and the required materials listed in the intake form.

Your launch date. Your expected launch date assumes you send your revision list, approve your website and pay the balance on time. If any of these happen later, your launch date moves, and I’ll confirm the new date by email.

If dates change. I’ll confirm any new dates by email, and that email updates your order confirmation.

Late materials or feedback. If materials or feedback are overdue, I’ll email you before pausing the project and releasing your reserved time. When you’re ready to continue, we’ll agree new dates around my availability. Your payment stays applied to the project unless you cancel.

If life gets in the way. If illness, a family emergency or another difficulty affects your project, please let me know. I’m happy to work out a new schedule with you.

Preview, revisions and launch

  1. Preview. When your website is ready, I’ll send you a private preview link.
  2. One consolidated revision round. Check everything carefully, including names, credits, agency details and links, and send me all your changes together in one list. Changes within the setup, such as wording, photo order and corrections, are included. Changes outside it, such as a different design, new pages or new features, are quoted separately.
  3. Approval. Once I’ve made your changes, I’ll ask you to approve your website for launch.
  4. Launch. Once you’ve approved it and paid the balance, I’ll connect your domain and launch your website, then send your edit link and password.

Fixing my own errors. If something I set up doesn’t work as described, for example a broken link or a page that doesn’t display properly, tell me within 30 days after launch and I’ll fix it at no charge. This 30-day period is separate from hosting and the running of your edit screen (see Hosting and technical responsibility). It doesn’t cover changes you make yourself or problems caused by outside services.

Your materials and permissions

  • Permission. You confirm you have the right to use everything you send me on your website, including photos, production stills, video, music in your reel and text. Photographers usually keep the copyright in their photos, so check that your agreement allows website use, and let me know if your photographer requires a credit.
  • Accuracy. You’re responsible for checking that your information is accurate, including your credits, training and representation.
  • Young actors. For actors under 19, the parent or guardian approves everything that goes on the website. I won’t publish a minor’s personal phone number, email address or home address. When you update contact details in your edit screen, use your agency’s details or a parent or guardian’s email, never the actor’s own.
  • Accounts in your name. Your domain, Formspree account and YouTube or Vimeo account are in your name, or a parent or guardian’s. You’re responsible for keeping them active, including renewing your domain.
  • Your edit screen. You’re responsible for what you publish through your edit screen, and for keeping your password safe. Everything you enter there can appear publicly on your website.

Hosting and technical responsibility

Hosting is currently included. While I offer it, hosting your website and keeping your edit screen running are included at no extra charge. This isn’t a promise to host your website for free forever.

If hosting becomes paid or stops. I’ll email you at least 60 days before the change. If hosting is becoming paid, the email will set out the proposed price and what it includes. You can then choose to pay or to move your website (see Leaving). I won’t charge you for hosting unless you agree to it. If you choose to move, don’t agree to pay by the date in my notice, or I stop hosting, your website comes off my hosting on that date. I’ll send you your website files before then.

Routine upkeep. While I host your website, I’m responsible for the routine technical running of my setup: the hosting account, the template your website is built on and your edit screen, working with Cloudflare and GitHub as they normally run. If a fault in my setup stops your website from displaying or your edit screen from saving, I’ll fix it at no charge within a reasonable time.

What isn’t included. Updating your content (you can do this yourself in your edit screen), help using your edit screen, changes you ask me to make, design changes, new features, and problems with accounts in your name, such as an expired domain. Hosting doesn’t include unlimited free repairs or support. Anything outside what I look after is quoted separately, subject to availability.

How your website runs. Your website is hosted in my account with Cloudflare, and your edit screen saves your changes through GitHub. Your contact form uses Formspree, your reel plays from YouTube or Vimeo, and your domain is registered with the company you chose. I don’t control these outside services. They can change their features, limits or prices, and they occasionally have outages.

Other changes I introduce. For any other change to how I host your website that affects you, I’ll give you at least 30 days’ notice by email where practicable, explaining what’s changing and your options.

Temporary outages. If Cloudflare, GitHub or another service your website uses has an outage, your website or edit screen may be unavailable until that service is restored. There’s nothing for me to repair, and there’s no charge.

Major provider changes. If a service ends a free plan, changes its terms or retires something your website relies on, it may give me little notice, or none. I’ll tell you as soon as I reasonably can and explain your options, which may include a new arrangement at a price I’ll quote, or moving your website.

No new charges without your agreement. If a service starts charging or changes significantly, I’ll explain the options before we agree any extra cost. I won’t add a charge without your agreement, and I can’t promise to cover all future costs of outside services myself. If you’d rather not pay for a service, the alternatives may include:

  • moving to a free plan or a different service, if a suitable one exists
  • removing the feature, for example replacing your contact form with your agent’s details
  • moving your website to another host (see Leaving)

Cancelling

Before work begins: a full refund. If you cancel by email before I start setting up your website, I’ll refund everything you’ve paid. Work usually begins on your agreed start date, and I’ll let you know when I start.

After work begins: the value of work completed, up to $100. If you cancel after I’ve started, I’ll deduct the reasonable value of the work I’ve actually completed, up to a maximum of $100, and refund the rest of what you’ve paid. This isn’t an automatic $100 fee: if I’ve done less work, I deduct less.

Your breakdown and refund. If I deduct anything, I’ll email you a short breakdown of the work completed and its value within 7 days of your cancellation. I’ll refund the remaining amount by Interac e-Transfer within 15 days of your cancellation.

Approval and final payment:

  • You only pay the balance after you’ve approved your website. Until then, you can cancel under the rules above.
  • If you approve your website but decide not to go ahead with the final payment and launch, the same rules apply: I deduct the value of work completed, up to $100, and refund the rest.
  • Once you’ve paid the balance and your website has launched, the setup is complete and this cancellation process no longer applies. My promise to fix my own errors (see Preview, revisions and launch) and your legal rights still apply.

If I can’t complete your website. If I can’t finish your website for any reason on my side, I’ll refund everything you’ve paid.

Your legal rights. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under BC consumer protection law, including any right to a full refund. For example, BC law may let you cancel and get a full refund if your website isn’t supplied within 30 days after the agreed date.

Leaving: moving your website

You can move your finished, paid-for website to another host and keep using it as your own actor website.

What you receive when you leave

  • a copy of your website’s published files: the pages, styles, scripts and images as they appear online
  • your content as files: everything you’ve saved in your edit screen, including your photos and stills, bio, credits, training, skills, personal and contact details, and reel and social links
  • your resume PDF, if you uploaded one

I’ll send these within 14 days of your request. The basic file handover is included. Help setting up another host, connecting your domain elsewhere or other migration work is quoted separately.

The files don’t include passwords, access tokens, account settings or any information about my other clients.

The edit screen doesn’t transfer. It’s built for my hosting setup and runs on my accounts, so it isn’t part of the files, and I can’t promise it would work unchanged on another host. After you move, updates are made by editing the files directly, or by whoever looks after your website.

Your domain stays yours, so you can point it to your new host.

When your website leaves my hosting. Once you confirm your website is working at its new host, I’ll remove it from my hosting. If you haven’t confirmed within 30 days of my sending your files, I’ll remove it then.

Who owns what

  • Your materials. You, or the people who gave you permission (such as photographers), keep ownership of the photos, video, text and other materials you supply.
  • My work. I keep ownership of my designs, templates, layouts, code and the edit screen, including any improvements I make to them. Paying for your website doesn’t transfer ownership of them to you.
  • Outside components. Fonts, software and services from others stay under their own licences.
  • Credit link. Your website’s footer includes a small “Website by Sullivan Park Design” link. If you’d prefer, I can remove it on request.

Your licence to use the design. Once you’ve paid in full, you may:

  • display the finished website for the actor named on your order confirmation
  • move it to another host
  • have necessary technical maintenance and content updates made that don’t redesign the template

What needs my written permission. Another designer or provider must get my written permission before modifying or adapting my template or design. Moving hosts doesn’t give anyone that permission.

What’s never allowed. You and other providers may not copy, distribute, sell or reuse my template or design to make websites for anyone else.

You’re always free to start fresh. You can commission a completely new design using your own content at any time.

Portfolio, privacy and other terms

Portfolio. Whether I can feature your website in my portfolio or marketing is a separate choice, made on its own form. Your answer doesn’t affect your website. For actors under 19, I need a parent or guardian’s written permission before featuring the website, the actor’s name or photos.

Privacy. I use the information you give me to build and support your website, as described in my privacy policy.

Responsibility. I’ll do the work with reasonable care and skill. I can’t guarantee auditions, bookings or search rankings, or that outside services will stay available or unchanged.

Changes to these terms. The version you accept applies to your order. If I update my terms, the new version applies to new orders, and to anything new we agree, such as paid hosting or extra work.

Contact and notices. Notices about your order are sent by email. You can reach me at hello@sullivanparkdesign.com.

BC law. These terms are governed by the laws of British Columbia.

Accepting these terms. Before you pay, I’ll email you your order confirmation and these terms to review. You accept by replying to confirm, then making your initial payment. I keep a copy of the version you accepted, and I’ll email you the complete agreed documents for your records within 2 business days.
